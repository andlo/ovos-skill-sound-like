# Sound credits

All bundled sounds in `sounds/animals/` are from
[bigsoundbank.com](https://bigsoundbank.com/), released under a
CC0 (public domain equivalent) license by Joseph SARDIN. Attribution
is not required by that license, but is included here anyway.

Some clips were trimmed to a shorter length for a snappier voice
response - the CC0/BigSoundBank license explicitly permits editing
and redistribution.

## Online fallback

Sounds fetched via the optional freesound.org fallback (not bundled,
not redistributed with this project) are filtered to CC0-licensed
results only - see `FREESOUND_LICENSE_FILTER` in `__init__.py`.
